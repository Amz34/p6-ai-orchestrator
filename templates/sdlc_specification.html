1. Executive Summary & Forensic Audit

Traditional project controls in heavy civil and megaproject construction rely on arduous manual workflows: drafting Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), calculating Critical Path Method (CPM) durations, assigning logic relationships, manual resource loading, budget balancing, and drafting lengthy Schedule Narrative Reports. For a typical commercial project, establishing an approved baseline takes between 7 to 21 working days and incurs thousands of dollars in planning engineering overhead.

By synthesizing the cutting-edge Model Context Protocol (MCP) server architecture (leveraging p6-mcp and direct P6 database hooks), we enable autonomous AI agents (powered by Google Gemini and Antigravity) to ingest Contract Terms, Bills of Quantities (BOQ), floor-by-floor construction sequences, and budget envelopes, then directly generate and mutate a fully resourced, logic-linked baseline directly inside Primavera P6 in under 10 minutes.

Key Architectural Breakthrough: Unlike legacy tools that require complex, error-prone manual Excel exports or XER file patching, the MCP gateway communicates directly with the active Primavera P6 EPPM instance or local SQLite backend. The planner opens P6, prompts the agent, and simply hits F5 (Refresh Database) and F9 (Run CPM Schedule Calculation).
